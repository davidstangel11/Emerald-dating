How to Get More Matches on Tinder: A 2026 Guide for Men

By David, founder of Emerald Dating · November 11, 2026 · 7 min read

Tinder is quietly working again. People are coming back, the app is choosing who sees whom more carefully, and a lot of men are still using it like it is 2016: swipe on everyone, write nothing, hope.

That approach now works against you. Here is how to get more matches on Tinder in 2026, based on what the app has actually changed this year and what it says it rewards.

What changed on Tinder this year

Two things matter for you.

More people are back. Match Group's CFO told Reuters that Tinder's US daily active users returned to annual growth in October, after global daily users returned to year over year growth in September for the first time in three and a half years. He credited the gains largely to better algorithms, with AI helping to identify more compatible users.

The algorithm is doing more of the choosing. At its Sparks 2026 keynote, Tinder introduced Chemistry, which Mediaweek describes as an AI layer serving curated daily recommendations based on personality signals, preferences and optional image analysis. It also announced Learning Mode, which adapts in real time to your activity, and an opt in Camera Roll Scan that suggests profile improvements. According to Global Dating Insights' write up of Match's Q3 2026 results, a new real time recommendation system is expected by the end of 2026.

Put simply: what you put on your profile and how you behave in the app now decide who Tinder shows you to. Volume alone buys less than it used to.

9 ways to get more matches on Tinder

1. Stop swiping right on everyone

If the system learns from your activity in real time, swiping right on every profile tells it nothing useful about you. Worse, it fills your match list with people who were never interested in the kind of man you are.

Swipe like you mean it. Like the profiles you would actually take on a date. You will see fewer matches from the wrong people and better recommendations over time.

2. Lead with one clear, close photo

Tinder's redesign gives photos a fuller display, so your first photo carries even more weight. Make it you, alone, face clearly visible, good light, no sunglasses, no hat shadow.

Then build out the rest: a full body shot, one of you doing something you actually do, one social photo where you are easy to spot. I walk through the full set in dating profile pictures for men.

3. Write a bio, and make it clear

Tinder has said plainly that vague bios are out. In its Dating Dictionary, as reported by Pedestrian via inkl, Tinder noted that "Looking for…" topped bios in 2024 because it promoted clarity. Its own Year in Swipe 2025 release found 60% of young singles calling for clearer communication around intentions.

So say what you want. "Looking for something real with someone who likes a plan" beats a blank space or a single emoji every time.

4. Use Tinder's "Rule of Three"

Tinder's own bio tip, from the same Dating Dictionary coverage: two aligned traits followed by one playful twist. The point is a bio that hooks and gives her an easy opener.

A few examples to adapt:

  • "Early runs, good coffee, and a strong opinion on the best taco in Austin."
  • "Work hard, travel when I can, still losing to my niece at Mario Kart."
  • "Into hiking, cooking and pretending I can dance at weddings."

Keep it true. If she cannot ask you about it on a first date, cut it.

5. Fill in your interests and date activities

Interests are one of the easiest signals for the app to read, and they double as conversation starters. In Tinder's Year in Swipe 2025 release, the top US bio interests were Foodie, Travel, Tattoos, Movies, Gym, 90s Kid, Music, Self Care, Sports and Outdoors, and the top date activities were Movie, Hiking, Concert, Drinks and Museum.

You do not need to copy the popular list. Pick the five that are actually you. Specific beats popular, and either beats leaving the section empty.

6. Verify your profile

Tinder is continuing the global rollout of Face Check, its liveness verification, and Tinder's planned video speed dating test is limited to photo verified users. Verification takes a minute. It removes one more reason for a cautious woman to pass on you, and it may open features that unverified profiles cannot use.

7. Try Double Date if it fits your life

This one surprises men. In Tinder's Year in Swipe 2025 release, women were almost three times more likely to like and match with a pair than with an individual profile, and Double Date chats saw 25% more messages per match than one on one chats. Match says Double Date has generated 30 million matches.

The honest caveat: the same release says nearly 85% of Double Date users are under 30. If you are 38, it may be thin in your area. If you have a single friend you trust, test it for two weeks. I covered how to turn a group plan into a one on one date in Tinder group hangouts explained.

8. Reply fast and keep it light

Tinder's advice here is simple: faster responses mean faster sparks, so keep messages quick, light and fun. A match you answer two days later is a match you have mostly lost.

You do not need to live in the app. Check twice a day, reply in one sitting, and move toward a plan within a few messages. If a match goes quiet, my post on getting ghosted on dating apps covers what to do next.

9. Use AI to sharpen your profile, not to write it

Tinder is leaning into AI, and the Camera Roll Scan is built to suggest profile improvements. Use tools like that to pick photos and spot gaps. Do not let them write a bio that sounds like nobody. Women can tell, and the AI on the other end is trying to match the real you.

What not to do

  • Do not reset your account to chase a new user boost. You lose your history and your matches, and you start the learning process over from zero.
  • Do not open with "hey." Comment on something specific in her profile and ask one easy question.
  • Do not pile up matches you never message. A long list of silent matches is wasted effort and tells the app nothing good.
  • Do not judge your profile on one weekend. Change one thing, give it a week, then compare.

How to run a one week Tinder reset

If you are busy, do it in this order:

  1. Day 1: Swap your first photo and cut any with sunglasses, group shots up front or old pictures.
  2. Day 2: Rewrite your bio with a clear "looking for" line plus a Rule of Three.
  3. Day 3: Add five honest interests and verify your profile.
  4. Days 4 to 7: Swipe selectively once a day, reply within a few hours, and ask for a date by the fifth or sixth message.

At the end of the week, count one number: dates booked. Matches are only useful if they turn into real plans.

The bottom line

Tinder in 2026 rewards clear profiles and real intent more than raw swiping. Pick your photos carefully, say what you want, be selective, and move fast once you match. That is how to get more matches on Tinder now, and better ones.

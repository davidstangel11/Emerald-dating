Halloween Date Ideas: 10 Plans That Turn Matches Into Dates

By David, founder of Emerald Dating · October 26, 2026 · 7 min read

Halloween lands on a Saturday this year, which makes this week one of the easiest of the fall to turn a match into a date. The problem is that most men either suggest the same drink at the same bar or say nothing and let the moment pass.

Here are Halloween date ideas that actually work for a first or second date, how to ask, and how to keep the night easy.

Why Halloween is a great week to ask her out

People are already in the mood to go out. The National Retail Federation's annual survey found that 74% of Americans plan to celebrate Halloween in 2026, up from 68% in 2019, with total spending expected to hit a record $13.5 billion. Half of consumers plan to wear a costume.

Singles are leaning in too. Eventbrite reported a 93% increase in Halloween dating experiences and a 159% jump in attendance, about four times the growth rate of holiday and Valentine's Day themed dating events. Searches for "singles Halloween" spiked 340%.

That same report cites Eventbrite's 2024 Dating Report, where 70% of men and 74% of women said a first date was a success if they had fun. Not if it was impressive. Not if it was expensive. Fun. Halloween gives you a built in reason to plan something more fun than a drink.

There is also some old psychology on your side. In a classic 1974 study by Dutton and Aron, men who crossed a shaky suspension bridge were significantly more likely to call a woman they met at the end of it than men who crossed a stable bridge. Part of the nervous energy got read as attraction. I would not build a dating strategy on one study from the 1970s, but a little shared excitement on a date never hurts.

How to ask (so she actually says yes)

After more than 10 years of helping men with dating, I can tell you the idea matters less than how you pitch it. A vague "we should hang out sometime" gets a vague answer. A specific plan gets a yes or a counter offer, and both move you forward.

Use this formula: the plan, a day, an easy out.

I'm going to the haunted house at the old armory Friday night. Come scream with me? If haunted houses aren't your thing, drinks after are on me either way.

It is specific, it is playful and it gives her an option that still ends in a date. Send it once you have had a few good messages back and forth, not after the first hello.

10 Halloween date ideas for this week

Pick one that fits where you are with her. A first date should be short and easy to leave. A second or third date can be bigger.

1. A haunted house or haunted trail

The classic for a reason. You have something to react to together, plenty to talk about afterwards and a natural moment to stand a little closer. Book timed tickets so you are not standing in line for an hour, then plan a nearby bar for after.

2. A ghost tour of your city

Almost every big city has a walking ghost or history tour this week. It is part sightseeing, part storytelling and only about 90 minutes long. That makes it a strong first date, because it has a built in end time and you can extend it with dinner if it is going well.

3. A Halloween bar crawl or themed cocktail bar

Plenty of cocktail bars run a Halloween menu or a pop up all month. Pick one great spot, not five. Costumes optional. A low key option is to go the Thursday before Halloween, when it is busy enough to feel fun but quiet enough to talk.

4. A singles costume party or event

This is the play if you are still working on getting matches to say yes. Singles Halloween events have grown fast, according to the Eventbrite data above. Go with a friend, talk to people and treat it as a way to meet women in person rather than through a screen.

5. A horror movie with a plan for after

A scary movie on its own is a weak first date, because you sit in the dark for two hours without talking. Fix that by putting the conversation on either side. Meet for a quick drink, catch a late showing of a classic, then argue about the ending over dessert.

6. A pumpkin patch or apple orchard

This is the best daytime option, especially if she is not into horror. Daylight, fresh air and a relaxed pace. It works well as a second date and gives you an easy reason to grab lunch at a nearby spot.

7. A pumpkin carving or cooking night

Better for a third date or later, once you know you get along. Pick up two pumpkins, a bottle of wine and some takeout. The NRF survey found 42% of people plan to carve pumpkins this year, so you will not be the only one. Keep it light and do not overthink the designs.

8. An escape room with a horror theme

Escape rooms show you how she thinks and how you work together, which is useful information early on. Most venues run a horror or Halloween themed room this time of year. Book for two, keep the stakes low and laugh when you get stuck.

9. A spooky dinner reservation

Some restaurants run Halloween tasting menus or dine in themed rooms. If you want a classic dinner date with a twist, this is it. Book early, because Halloween weekend fills up fast in most big cities.

10. A costume contest at a local bar

If you both like dressing up, a bar costume contest gives you a goal for the night and something to laugh about. Eventbrite saw costume contest events and attendance grow sharply. Coordinate costumes only if you are already a few dates in. On a first date, matching outfits is a lot.

What to skip

Some Halloween plans quietly sink a good match.

  • Huge house parties on a first date. You will spend the night shouting over music and losing each other in the crowd.
  • Anything over two or three hours for date one. Leave her wanting more. A short, fun date beats a long, tiring one.
  • Costumes that could offend. If you have to ask whether it is too much, it is. Pick something fun and easy. The NRF's top adult picks are witches, vampires, Batman, Spider-Man and ghosts. Simple works.
  • Pressuring her to dress up. Make costumes optional. Some people love it and some people hate it.
  • Overspending. Fun beats fancy. You do not need a $200 tasting menu to make a good impression.

Make the most of it on the apps this week

Halloween is a great excuse to update your profile and restart quiet conversations.

  • Use it as an opener. "Important question: haunted house or pumpkin patch?" is easy to answer and leads straight into a plan.
  • Revive old matches. A match who went quiet two weeks ago may say yes to a fun, specific plan. Send one light message with an idea, not a "hey stranger."
  • Add a seasonal photo or prompt, but only one. A good photo of you at a pumpkin patch is fine. Six Halloween photos is not.

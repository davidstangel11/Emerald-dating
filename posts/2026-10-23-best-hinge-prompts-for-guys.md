## Why your Hinge prompts matter more than you think

Hinge is built around prompts. Every profile needs three written answers, and those answers are what people like and comment on. That makes them the main way a conversation starts.

The data backs this up. According to [Hinge research reported by TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/08/hinges-new-ai-feature-helps-daters-start-better-convos-moving-beyond-boring-small-talk), 72% of Hinge daters are more inclined to consider someone when a like comes with a message, and daters who include a comment with their likes are twice as likely to arrange a date. A good prompt is what makes a comment easy to write.

Most people find this part hard. Hinge cited a user survey showing that [63% of daters struggle with what to write on their profiles](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/15/hinge-new-ai-feature-determines-if-your-prompt-response-is-too-basic/) and often fall back on stereotypical answers. That is your opening. If most profiles are generic, a specific one stands out fast.

Hinge's Director of Relationship Science, Logan Ury, put the goal well in the company's [profile refresh guide](https://hinge.co/press/profile-refresh-guide): "Help them imagine what it's like to date you by painting a vivid picture of your life."

## The rule behind every good prompt answer

After more than 10 years of helping men with dating, I judge every prompt answer with one question: **is it easy to reply to?**

A strong answer does three things:

- **It is specific.** A real place, a real habit, a real opinion. "I like travel" says nothing. "I still think about a breakfast taco I had in Austin in 2023" says something.
- **It shows a side of you.** Humor, ambition, warmth or a little vulnerability. Spread these across your three prompts instead of repeating one.
- **It leaves a hook.** A question, a light challenge or an opinion she can push back on. Hinge's own research found that [half of daters feel bored when they get a one word opener](https://hinge.co/press/video-prompts-prompt-polls/), so give her something better to say than "haha."

## The 9 best Hinge prompts for guys (with example answers)

These are real prompts from Hinge's list. The example answers are templates to show the structure. Swap in your own details, because a borrowed answer reads as borrowed.

### 1. "Together, we could"

This is the best prompt on the app for a busy man, because it puts a date idea straight into her head.

> Together, we could find the best old fashioned in the city. I have a shortlist of three. You can break the tie.

It is specific, it suggests a real plan and it invites her to join in.

### 2. "I'm weirdly attracted to"

Playful and easy to answer. Keep it light and a little unexpected.

> I'm weirdly attracted to people who have a strong opinion about where to get the best pizza. Defend your pick.

### 3. "The way to win me over is"

Skip the generic "be kind." Name something small and real.

> The way to win me over is to recommend a book and then actually ask me what I thought of it.

### 4. "Two truths and a lie"

A classic for a reason: it is a built in game. The trick is making all three believable and interesting.

> I once missed a flight because I was finishing a chess game. I can cook a proper risotto. I have never seen a Star Wars movie.

She will guess. That is a conversation.

### 5. "My most controversial opinion is"

Pick something low stakes and fun to argue about. Never politics, never anything that makes you sound bitter.

> My most controversial opinion is that breakfast for dinner is better than breakfast for breakfast.

### 6. "I geek out on"

This is where you show passion without bragging about your job. Enthusiasm is attractive, and detail is what makes it real.

> I geek out on espresso. I have spent an embarrassing amount of time adjusting my grinder, so I will judge your coffee order (kindly).

### 7. "A life goal of mine"

This one lets you show ambition and direction, which matters to a lot of women looking for something serious. Keep it human, not a résumé.

> A life goal of mine is to run a marathon in every decade of my life. One down. Looking for someone to cheer at the finish line of the next one.

### 8. "My simple pleasures"

Great for warmth. It balances out a profile that otherwise leans on career and travel.

> My simple pleasures: a long Sunday walk with no plan, a good burger after a workout and my mom's Sunday call that always runs long.

### 9. "One thing I'd love to know about you is"

This prompt is literally a question for her, which makes replying effortless. Make the question specific and fun.

> One thing I'd love to know about you is your go to order at a diner at 1am. It tells me everything.

## Prompts to skip (or answer very carefully)

Some prompts set you up to sound generic or negative. Hinge's guide lists the same pattern: avoid one word answers, generic answers and clichés, and change any prompt that is not working.

- **"The best way to ask me out is by"** almost always gets the cliché answer "asking me out." Hinge's own Prompt Feedback tool was designed to flag answers like that. If you use it, add a real suggestion.
- **"I'm looking for"** tends to produce vague lists. If relationship goals matter to you, say it in one clear, warm sentence somewhere else.
- **"A boundary of mine is"** and anything that reads like a list of demands. Negativity on a profile costs likes even when the point is reasonable.
- **"My therapist would say I"** works for some people, but it is hard to land without oversharing. Only use it if the answer is genuinely funny.

## How to pick your three

Think of your prompts as a set, not three separate answers.

1. **One that suggests a date.** "Together, we could" or "First round is on me if."
2. **One that shows personality.** Humor, a quirky opinion or a game like "Two truths and a lie."
3. **One that shows depth.** A goal, a passion or a simple pleasure.

Then use the extras. Hinge also offers a 30 second voice prompt and prompt polls, which highlight three things about you, per the [profile refresh guide](https://hinge.co/press/profile-refresh-guide). A short, relaxed voice prompt is one of the fastest ways to come across as a real person instead of a set of photos.

## Use Hinge's built in tools, then test

Hinge gives you two free ways to improve. **Prompt Feedback** reviews your answers privately and tells you whether to go deeper or make a small change. It does not write the answer for you, which is a good thing. **Top Photo** tests your pictures and moves the most popular one to the front automatically.

If you want help drafting, AI tools can be useful for brainstorming, as we covered in [using AI for dating apps](https://blog.emerald.dating/using-ai-for-dating-apps/). Just make sure the final words sound like you. And a friend's view is often the best editor of all, which is exactly what [Hinge Friend's Take](https://blog.emerald.dating/hinge-friends-take-tips-for-men/) is for.

Then treat your profile like any other test. Change one prompt at a time, give it a week and watch which answer gets the most comments. Comments, not likes, are the number that tells you a prompt is working.
